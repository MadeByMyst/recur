Returns and repairs

Free both ways, and repairs don't expire.

Returns

Send anything back within 30 days, unworn and with the tags on, and we'll refund you in full. The return label is free and it's already in the box. Refunds land within five working days of the parcel reaching us.

Repairs

We'll repair anything you bought from us, for free, for as long as you own it: split seams, worn cuffs, loose buttons, small holes. Email us a photo, we'll send a free label, and it'll be back with you in about two weeks.

If it's past saving, we'll recycle it and give you 20% off a new one.

Questions

The ones we get asked most.

Why don't you do limited drops?

Because the best part of finding a piece you love is being able to buy it again. Drops make clothes feel urgent. We'd rather they felt dependable.

How do I know when something's back?

Every sold-out size shows its return date in the shop, and the Coming back list on the shop page has all of them. Leave your email on the product and we'll write once, on the day it's back.

Do the sizes run true?

Mostly. The hoodie, crew and overshirt are boxy on purpose, so size down if you like a closer fit. Everything else is true to size, and the size guide above has exact measurements.

Will it shrink?

Everything is pre-shrunk. Wash at 30°C, dry flat, and it'll stay the size you bought. If it doesn't, send it back.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, as long as it hasn't left the studio. Email us with your order number and we'll sort it out.

Do you ship to my country?

Almost certainly. If your country isn't in the list at checkout, email us and we'll find a way.

Is RECUR a real brand?

No. RECUR is a concept store designed and built by MadeByMyst for a portfolio. The clothes, Walter and Jesse, and the factory are made up, and the checkout never takes any money.
